# AI Capabilities

**Location**: <img src="../../../../assets/Homepage_settings_cog-account_settings_.png" alt="" data-size="line"> → **Global Settings** → **AI**

This section lets you control which AI-powered capabilities are enabled for your tenant.

## Allow AI Usage

The **Allow AI Usage** toggle is the master control for AI-powered capabilities within the platform. When enabled, dependent AI features become available for configuration and use. When disabled, all AI-dependent capabilities are turned off tenant-wide, regardless of how they are configured individually. For an example of an AI-dependent feature, see SAST Findings Analysis.
